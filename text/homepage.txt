From grief, rises bread.

We bake to heal. Every loaf tells a story.

Welcome to Bread & Bone Bakery.

We are a small, family-run bakery in Woodstock, Cape Town. What started as one woman's journey through grief has become a community hub for healing, connection, and hope.

Our Speciality Loaves:

Amina's Sunrise Loaf - R45
A golden sourdough with sunflower seeds and honey. Named after Lungile's daughter, Amina, whose nickname was "Sunshine."

Grief & Grace Croissant - R35
Buttery, flaky, filled with dark chocolate and a pinch of sea salt. The salt represents tears. The chocolate represents finding sweetness in pain.

Sipho's Seed Bread - R50
A dense, nutty loaf with pumpkin, flax, and chia seeds. Named after Lungile's husband, Sipho – strong and full of hope.
