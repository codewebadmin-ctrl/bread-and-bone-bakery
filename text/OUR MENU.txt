OUR MENU

Every item has a story. Every bite has meaning.

SOURDOUGH LOAVES

Amina's Sunrise Loaf - R45
A golden sourdough with sunflower seeds and honey. Named after Lungile's daughter, Amina, whose nickname was "Sunshine." The sunflower seeds are her favourite flower.

Sipho's Seed Bread - R50
A dense, nutty loaf with pumpkin, flax, and chia seeds. Named after Lungile's husband, Sipho – the rock of the family.

PASTRIES

Grief & Grace Croissant - R35
Buttery, flaky, filled with dark chocolate and a pinch of sea salt. The salt represents tears. The chocolate represents finding sweetness in pain.

COOKIES

Staffroom Cookies - R25
Simple sugar cookies with heart-shaped sprinkles. A tribute to the nurses at Red Cross Hospital who became family.

Sleepy Head Cookies - R28
Oatmeal cookies with chamomile and honey. Perfect for winding down at night.
