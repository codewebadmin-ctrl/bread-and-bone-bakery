OUR STORY

From Grief to Purpose

In 2022, Lungile Ndlovu's life changed forever. Her 8-year-old daughter, Amina, was diagnosed with a rare heart condition and admitted to the Red Cross Children's Hospital in Cape Town.

During the long, sleepless nights at her daughter's bedside, Lungile found solace in the hospital's communal kitchen. She began baking bread – not for profit, but for peace.

The rhythmic kneading, the warmth of the oven, the smell of rising dough – it was the only thing that kept her hands busy while her heart was breaking.

The hospital staff and other parents noticed her loaves. They asked for more. She baked through the pain. She baked through the grief.

When Amina passed away in early 2023, Lungile realized that baking had saved her life. It had given her purpose when she felt she had none.

The Birth of Bread & Bone

With the support of her husband Sipho, Lungile opened Bread & Bone in Woodstock, Cape Town, in late 2023.

The name is a tribute:
"Bread" for what she baked, and "Bone" for the fragility of life.

What started as a coping mechanism has become a community hub – a place where people come not just for bread, but for healing, connection, and hope.

Mission Statement:
"We bake to heal. Every loaf tells a story. Every customer is part of our family. We don't just sell bread – we sell hope, memory, and the belief that even in the darkest times, something beautiful can rise."

Vision Statement:
To become a nationally recognized brand that uses baking as a vehicle for grief counselling, community healing, and social impact – while remaining a small, authentic, and deeply personal bakery.
